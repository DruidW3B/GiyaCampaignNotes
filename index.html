AppendixCharacters and Factions▾

Player Characters

Hella

▾
  • Jafar tried to use his charisma on her at the end of Session I and failed. When the party later found him attacking Duchess Marine, Hella (Red Jasmin) landed the final blow.
  • Bilogis of the Dark Pantheon is assumed to be affecting her.
  • In an effort to save Prickly from himself, she let everyone know, including Prickly, that the two of them are married now. Prickly agreed.
  • Jackson the bull is her dear friend; the damage he took in the battle for Darathan's lair left her upset.
  • Gave Prickly a poem to soothe his worries over his errors in combat.
  • Arose from her slumber wearing an odd cloak covered in mushrooms, with a strange rune. When she tries it on she senses whispers from it and feels very calm. Orphelia, Oth, and Lance know it has something to do with a pale wraith-like lady.
  • The cloak is white velvet fabric, made for her by the Phantom Queen, a deity who makes them in her grove.

Eris Thornveil

▾
  • Saval of the Dark Pantheon is assumed to be affecting her.
  • Got her first sword shard in Session 2.5, starting her on her quest to retrieve them all.
  • At the Tea House, explained that her sword gave her some insight.
  • Found letters in Prince Darathan's desk on the second floor of his lair, and kept them to herself until Session XI.
  • Holds Darathan's notes: shipping documents and shipments talking about the obsidian, with no incriminating evidence relating to Bilogis.

Lance

▾
  • A large, red, menacing figure peering down from the clouds is watching him specifically. The party identified it in Session III as Volmol of the Dark Pantheon, who is assumed to be affecting him.
  • Volmol is also what the goblins' sacrifices were meant to summon.
  • Knew, along with Orphelia and Oth, that Hella's cloak has something to do with a pale wraith-like lady.

Jeros

▾
  • Picked up the goblin shaman's staff and waved it around, nearly causing a total party kill. His chaotic streak set the tone for how the party has treated him since.
  • Imotet of the Dark Pantheon is assumed to be affecting him, although that is not specifically said in game.
  • Stole Orphelia's parrot, leading to his outcasting. There is no news about the parrot.
  • Was mad at his dad for not letting him go out to sea, and Darathan the Black decided to take him on board his crew. After or during negotiations he lost his memory, but as time passed he realized he committed his crime.
  • His memories, surfaced at the Tea House, show a woman at Sir Darathan's side doing some witchcraft, and Darathan leaving with him while he was incapacitated under a spell. An enchantress in Darathan's lair is responsible for the erasing of his memories and his conviction for the stolen parrot.
  • The party went to the pirate lords to advocate for him and didn't do well, but the pirate council declared him innocent of his crimes and upgraded his father to pirate lord.
  • When Sir Darathan's broadside engulfed his father's ship in magical Greek fire, he was forced to choose which parent to save. He chose his mother while doing everything he could to save his father as well, and pulled him out horribly burned and in a sort of coma.
  • Cares for his father at Orphelia's tavern, where his little brother is also staying.

Ezekiel — left the campaign in Session IV

▾
  • Vugak of the Dark Pantheon is assumed to be affecting him.
  • Camping on the way back from the goblin camp, he was visited by a mysterious woman offering instant fame and fortune in exchange for his eternal soul: The Collector.
  • One-punched so hard in the great arena fight that he left the campaign.

Oth — new to the party, first logged in Session XII

▾
  • Knew, along with Orphelia and Lance, that Hella's cloak has something to do with a pale wraith-like lady.

Prickly Khactuz — Druid · played by Web

▾
  • The party's druid. Befriended Jackson the bull just before the goblin camp, and went on a first hunt with Sydney, the party's baby great white shark.
  • Izmataz of the Dark Pantheon is assumed to be affecting no one, and Prickly is positive he is not being affected. The DM laughed for some unrelated reason.
  • Izmataz later visited him in a dream as a magical bird-humanoid, offered knowledge of his father and his true form, and recommended he see Azec Aroman at the Tower.
  • Found out that Orphelia is his long-lost adopted sister.
  • Married to Hella, as of Hella letting everyone know. He agreed.
  • Reunited with his father Khozi, now in his final form as a magical tree, and is investigating how he may also achieve this form of druid nirvana.
  • Founded a network of espionage and spycraft through his trusty seagulls, which found Darathan's black ship.
  • Pushed for talking with the rat folk because of their cuteness, scouted Darathan's lair in spider form, and had the party spare the juvenile hydra.

Factions

The Dark Pantheon — six dark gods

▾
  • 70 years ago, six dark gods attempted to break into the world. They normally are solo, but this time they joined together to have the planet as a playground of destruction.
  • It's a possibility they have agents on the planet. Each is responsible for and connected to many of the players.
  • Saval: temptress, red horns, ambition. Assumed to be affecting Eris.
  • Bilogis: famine, green. Assumed to be affecting Hella. The root cause of the plague on the Plague Island.
  • Vugak: clown, laughter and trickery. Assumed to be affecting Ezekiel.
  • Volmol: the big red one, war and destruction. Assumed to be affecting Lance, and the god the goblins' sacrifices were meant to summon.
  • Izmataz: god of knowledge, by his own word the god of unrelenting knowledge. Assumed to be affecting no one. Visited Prickly's dream as a magical bird-humanoid, suffering from a severe auditory hallucination, constantly hearing his name and conversations echoing in his mind.
  • Imotet: god of death. Assumed to be affecting Jeros, although that is not specifically said in game.

The Dark Fire Circus

▾
  • Where the party's story began: gambling and carnival games, then a show in the Big Top that ended when goblin raiders swarmed it.
  • Jyasinta Helvitar, the fortune teller, teleported herself to safety during the raid.
  • Elbafore Critterspeaker performs wild magics with his fish in a bubble, Jeramiah. The goblins held him in a cave near their camp.
  • Lady Alrina Kazadina, a powerful druid who speaks to animals, performs with her mammoth.
  • Other proprietors include Dr Simon Corvus, a potion seller who seems a little off, and Theradeas Toplledoppolos, one of the brothers who own the Gooey Pork Tavern chain.

The Goblins

▾
  • Raiders who swarmed the Big Top of the Dark Fire Circus and made off with prisoners.
  • Their leader looks massive by goblin standards, with glowing red eyes.
  • Their sacrifices were meant to summon not the spider god everyone assumed, but Volmol of the Dark Pantheon.
  • The party fought through their camp with Jackson the bull and killed the goblin shaman. Jeros picked up the shaman's staff, and the party took the ritual book.

L'Equip — a bastion near the Dark Fire Circus

▾
  • Its knights arrived to cover the party's retreat from the goblin raid. Joseph Kiliman is its Knight Commander.
  • Duchess Marine hinted that all is not what it seems in L'Equip.
  • Jafar was guilty of treachery. The party found him in the throne room attacking Duchess Marine and killed him, with Hella landing the final blow.

Fizzle Industries

▾
  • Told the party more about the cannon they were after: it was being held by a lady in red, who turned out to be Orphelia.

The Pirate Lord Council

▾
  • The eight pirate lords: Capitan Del Herro Nochas, Capitan Emilia Von Drakkenberg, Kairi Namujima, the Gentleman Pirate, Pirate Capitan Osiris, Orphelia, Pirate Prince Darathan the Black, and Gozlock.
  • The party went to the different pirate lords to advocate for Jeros and didn't really do well.
  • In the reception area before the council: First Mate Nigel Philloge, First Mate Jean Luc, First Mate Zinzu Harikumi, First Mate Gentleman Jack Gallaher, Zul'Aram, and First Mate Ahmose.
  • Inside the council sat Marcus Wraithbone, along with the other pirate lords aside from Prince Darathan.
  • Declared Jeros innocent of his crimes and upgraded his father to pirate lord.

The Rat Folk

▾
  • Rat-like humanoids running a secret mining operation beneath the tide pool, under a commander named Sloppy Crookshanks.
  • They are after a green shining rock, which is very hazardous to the touch and, when consumed, gives the rats powerful abilities.
  • Persuaded to join the battle against the dark elves, with access to the green stones as payment.

The Dark Elves — Darathan the Black's mining operation

▾
  • A slave mining operation in the Tide Pool Dungeon, with a frightening number of slaves and soldiers working for Darathan the Black.
  • They were mining obsidian with red in it; Darathan's notes mention shipments of the obsidian.
  • The tunnels to their lair pass a pool of dead bodies, the domain of a very large basilisk or other large serpent-like creature.
  • Kept a juvenile hydra that did a ton of damage in the battle. The party did not kill it, on behalf of Prickly's wishes.
  • Stormed by the party and the rat folk from the Tide Pool Dungeon while Orphelia attacked from above ground. Prince Darathan got away, and the slaves freed were people who had been going missing around town.

The Barbarians

▾
  • Extremely confrontational dwellers of the cavern network; an uneasy annoyance for Sloppy Crookshanks.
  • The party investigated and fought them in the Tide Pool Dungeon to look into the plague plotline.

The Red Crusaders

▾
  • Methias Gank, a monster/witch hunter, works with them.
  • They are looking for a hag in the woods, and believe the spiders are connected.
  • A bit on the racist side, favoring humans.
  • Hella wants to talk about their religion; the party got led to a tent.

NPCs

Darathan the Black — Pirate Prince, one of the eight pirate lords

▾
  • Also called Prince Darathan and Sir Darathan.
  • His black ship was seen dumping the bodies of dead slaves into the ocean, which washed ashore and became the onset of the plague on the Plague Island.
  • Took Jeros on board his crew, and left with him while Jeros was incapacitated under a spell.
  • Ran the dark elf slave mining operation in the Tide Pool Dungeon from an office on its 2nd level, along with the enchantress.
  • Got away when the lair was stormed. The notes Eris took from his desk hold shipping documents and obsidian shipments, with no incriminating evidence relating to Bilogis.
  • Was not among the pirate lords at the council that cleared Jeros. Afterward his ship fired a broadside from a long way off, engulfing Jeros's father's ship in magical Greek fire.

The Enchantress — at Darathan's side

▾
  • Seen with Darathan in an office on the 2nd level of the dark elf lair.
  • Responsible for the erasing of Jeros's memories and his conviction for the stolen parrot.
  • In the memories surfaced at the Tea House, a woman at Sir Darathan's side did some witchcraft before Darathan left with Jeros under a spell.

Orphelia — pirate lord and Prickly's adopted sister

▾
  • A powerful pirate captain, the lady in red, and one of the eight pirate lords.
  • Prickly's long-lost adopted sister.
  • The recipient of the cannon the party needed to find, as they learned from the royal lady.
  • Jeros stole her parrot, which led to his outcasting.
  • Keeps a tavern where the party settles. Jeros's little brother stays there, and she took Jeros's burned father there after the broadside.
  • Sent the party to investigate a suspicious tide pool, greenlit the battle with the dark elves, and attacked from above ground while the party and rat folk attacked from the Tide Pool Dungeon.

The Collector — a nemesis-like figure

▾
  • Appeared to Ezekiel as a mysterious woman offering instant fame and fortune in exchange for his eternal soul.
  • A nemesis-like figure for the party.

Jackson — the bull

▾
  • Befriended by Prickly just before the goblin camp, where he proved a formidable asset, slaying many goblins himself.
  • Hella's dear friend. Damaged in the battle for Darathan's lair, but more than eager to keep fighting, and confirmed he is happy and fighting beside his friends by his own will.

Broxagar — the Sheriff

▾
  • Had a nice tea party with the party. Prickly left him spiders to tend to the pests in his beloved garden.

Khozi — Prickly's father

▾
  • Reached his final form as a magical tree, just by sitting around many critters. What kind of tree is unknown.
  • Izmataz offered Prickly knowledge of him and his true form, and pointed to Azec Aroman at the Tower to find out more about his state.

Sydney — baby great white shark

▾
  • Joined the party in Session VII.
  • Went on a first hunt with Prickly.

Jeros's Family

▾
  • Jeros was mad at his dad for not letting him go out to sea; Darathan the Black then took him on board his crew.
  • The pirate council upgraded his father to pirate lord. As his father's ship was being set into the water, Sir Darathan's broadside engulfed it in magical Greek fire.
  • Jeros chose to save his mother while doing everything he could for his father, who was pulled out horribly burned and in a sort of coma. Jeros cares for him at Orphelia's tavern.
  • His little brother is staying with Orphelia in her tavern.

Emilia Von Drakkenberg — Capitan, one of the eight pirate lords

▾
  • With the use of the tea, she can peer into Jeros's mind and potentially act as a witness.

Azec Aroman — at the Tower

▾
  • Izmataz recommends Prickly see him to find out more about his father's state.

The Missing Elven Princess

▾
  • A detective's letter detailed her disappearance.
  • The party accepted the quest to find her in the Ironwield Forest.

Sal — a wizard

▾
  • The party ran into him, along with a member of the queen's guard, after fighting some spiders near the Ironwield Forest.

The Phantom Queen — a deity

▾
  • Makes white velvet fabric in her grove, and made Hella's mushroom-covered cloak for her.

Methias Gank — monster/witch hunter

▾
  • Works with the Red Crusaders, who are looking for a hag in the woods.

Session IThe Carnival (DM Notes)▾
TL;DR

The party's first night out, a visit to the Dark Fire Circus that turns to chaos when goblin raiders storm the Big Top. Knights of L'Equip cover the retreat, and the party glimpses a massive, red-eyed goblin leader before choosing whether to leave with Knight Commander Joseph Kiliman or chase the goblins and their prisoners. -Claude

Party at level 1.

The Dark Fire Circus

  • The party starts at the Dark Fire Circus, following a brief introduction of how and why each character ended up there, then eases in with gambling and carnival games.
  • The attractions and their proprietors:
    • Food from the Nobleshnap brothers of the Gooey Pork Tavern franchise
    • Fortune telling by Jyasinta Helvitar
    • Ring Da BellZ, run by Azlugg the Mighty
    • Target Practice, run by Grizzle Tooth Snagger
    • Trissinda's Tea Room, serving tea with an optional leaf reading
    • Shipple Shabangs' Find the Fairy Cup game
    • The Wheel of Fortune, run by Theradeas Toplledoppolos, one of the Toplledoppolos brothers who own the Gooey Pork Tavern chain
    • Dr Simon Corvus's potion stall, whimsical potions from a man who seems a little off

The Big Top

  • Once the party tires of the games, they are ushered into the Big Top: clowns, animal tamers, acrobats, and the wild magics of Elbafore Critterspeaker and his fish in a bubble, Jeramiah.
  • Elbafore announces the arrival of Lady Alrina Kazadina, a powerful druid who speaks to animals to entertain the crowd.

The Raid

  • Toward the end of the show, noises rise outside and the Big Top is swarmed by goblin raiders. The party and a few NPCs either flee or stand and fight.
  • If Lady Alrina falls, her mammoth turns feral and attacks anyone nearby unless Elbafore can calm it.
  • The party is nearly overrun until knights from the nearby bastion of L'Equip arrive to cover their retreat.

The Escape

  • As they leave, they glimpse Jyasinta teleporting herself to safety, and see the goblin leader: massive for a goblin, glowing red eyes, screaming orders to his subordinates.
  • The party can either leave with Knight Commander Joseph Kiliman or chase the goblins, who are making off with prisoners.

Ended session at level 1.

Session IIJackson the Bull Kills Goblins (DM Notes)▾
TL;DR

Chasing the goblin shaman to free the circus prisoners, the party befriends a bull named Jackson who tears through the goblin camp. They rescue Elbafore and learn the sacrifices aim to summon not a spider god but one of The Dark Pantheon, the red giant watching Lance, later known as Volmol. Ezekiel meets The Collector on the road home, and after the shaman falls, Jeros nearly wipes the party with its staff. Back at the fortress, they later expose and kill the treacherous Jafar, with Hella (Red Jasmin) landing the final blow, and Eris claims her first sword shard. -Claude

Party at level 2.

Before the Hunt

  • After Session 1, the party decides to chase down the goblin shaman to save any prisoners taken from the circus.
  • Each of them experiences a vision or dream involving a parent, a loved one, or a darker entity entirely.
  • Before leaving, they can speak with Duchess Marine, who hints that all is not what it seems in L'Equip. Talking with several NPCs turns up clues about who and what is causing trouble in the fortress.
  • Just before reaching the camp, the party's druid, Prickly Khactuz, befriended a bull named Jackson.

The Goblin Camp

  • The party approaches the goblin camp and is thrown straight into a fight as goblins swarm them. Jackson the bull proves a formidable asset, slaying many goblins himself.
  • After the battle, they can rescue Elbafore from a nearby cave, who helps heal the party before they move on to face the shaman.
  • They have a chance to save some of the carnival folk.

The Shaman

  • A vision reveals what the goblins are trying to summon with their sacrifices. It is not the spider god everyone assumed, but one of The Dark Pantheon: the god watching Lance specifically, a large, red, menacing figure peering down from the clouds. The party later identified it in Session 3 as Volmol.

The Journey Back

  • Camping on the way back, Ezekiel is visited by a mysterious woman offering instant fame and fortune in exchange for his eternal soul. When he refuses, she reveals herself as The Collector, a nemesis-like figure for the party.

Aftermath

  • The party succeeded in killing the goblin shaman.
  • Jeros picked up the shaman's staff and waved it around, nearly causing a total party kill and coming close to killing Lance and Eris. His chaotic streak here set the tone for how the party treated Jeros from then on.
  • They also took the ritual book, though the DM's note trails off on what Lance did with it.

Session 2.5 (Red Jasmin and Jafar Scene)

  • These notes are written by Web, so they are exactly how this all went down. In no way are any of these details incorrect. (insert sarcasm)
  • The party had a brief conversation with Jafar at the end of Session 1, where he tried to use his charisma on Hella and failed, leaving the party sketched out by him.
  • After Session 2, the party gathered more evidence that he is guilty of treachery and searched the castle for him, finding him in the throne room attacking Duchess Marine. The party killed him, with Hella (Red Jasmin) landing the final blow while Prickly, in parrot form, forced crackers into his mouth, taunting him: "Do you want a cracker, huh? Polly want a cracker!"
  • Eris Thornveil got her first sword shard, starting her on her quest to retrieve them all.

Ended session at level 2.

Ezekiel is punched clean out of the campaign, Hella declares herself married to Prickly, and the party leaves with Orphelia. -Claude

Party at level 3.

  • They met Broxagar the Sheriff and had a nice tea party with him.
  • Prickly left Broxagar spiders to tend to the pests in his beloved garden.
  • The party also meets with Fizzle Industries and learns more about the cannon they were after, being held by a lady in red.
  • Prickly found out some plants are fake and horribly kept.
  • Jeros gambles.
  • There is the great arena fight where Ezekiel is one-punched so hard he left the campaign.

## Session 12: White Velvet
> **TL;DR:** Hella wakes wearing an odd cloak covered in mushrooms and marked with a strange rune; trying it on, she senses whispers and feels very calm, and Orphelia, Oth, and Lance tie it to a pale wraith-like lady. After a fight with spiders near the Ironwield Forest, the party runs into a wizard named Sal and a member of the queen's guard, and learns the cloak is white velvet fabric made for her by the Phantom Queen, a deity who makes them in her grove. They also meet Methias Gank, a monster/witch hunter working with the Red Crusaders, who are looking for a hag in the woods; Hella wants to talk about their religion, and they get led to a tent. -Claude
> **Level:** 5

### Waking Up and Checking Out the Cloak
- Hella arises from her slumber with an odd cloak on. It's covered in mushrooms and has a strange rune.
- She senses there is something magical about it.
- Orphelia, Oth, and Lance all roll well and know this has something to do with a pale wraith-like lady.
- When she tries it on, she senses whispers from the cloak and she feels very calm.

### Near the Ironwield Forest

- The Phantom Queen is a deity that has made it for her. It's called white velvet fabric. She makes them in her grove.

- The spiders leave iron shards in their web naturally, though they themselves are monstrosities.

### The Red Crusaders
- Methias Gank is a monster/witch hunter working with the Red Crusaders.
